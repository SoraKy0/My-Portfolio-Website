Kaizen Today
https://github.com/SoraKy0/kaizen-today

Kaizen Today is a personal project I'm currently working on. It's a chill, low-pressure social space where people can talk about their day-to-day lives, share how they're feeling, and work towards their longer-term goals through regular check-ins.

I'm building it because I've always felt that a lot of social media can be pretty toxic and overwhelming. I wanted to make something that feels more relaxed, somewhere you can just talk about the little things going on in your day without everything having to be polished, impressive, or performative.

Kaizen Today is being built with React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with an Express backend and PostgreSQL planned for the database.
